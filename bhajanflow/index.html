Hari Sumiran (BhajanFlow)

A calm, local-first devotional audio app. It plays your daily bhajan routine from your own audio files, keeps your place in long series, and reminds you when it is time to listen.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

Daily plan

Opening tracks, an ongoing Series (for example a Ramayan path) and Closing tracks, played in order every day.

Remembers progress

Each Series moves forward only after you actually finish a track. Missing a day never loses your place.

Your own audio

Plays audio files you already own, from your device or from Google Drive files and folders you choose.

Reminders

Gentle notifications at the times you set, so the daily session is not forgotten.

Works offline

Everything works on the device. Internet is needed only for the optional Google Drive features.

Optional sync

Keep progress and routines in step between your phone and PC through a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.

How Google sign-in is used

Signing in with Google is optional. The app uses it only to open audio from your Google Drive and, if you turn it on, to sync your settings and progress between your own devices. The developer runs no server, and your data never passes through one. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.
