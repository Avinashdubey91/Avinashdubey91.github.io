Privacy Policy

App: Hari Sumiran (BhajanFlow) · Developer: Avinash Kumar Dubey · Last updated: 4 October 2026

In short: your data stays on your device and in your own Google account. The developer runs no server, does not collect your data, shows no ads, uses no analytics, and never sells or shares your data.

1. Who we are

Hari Sumiran (also called BhajanFlow) is a devotional audio app for Android and Windows, made by Avinash Kumar Dubey. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Contact: avipgdac091@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps its working data in a private database on your own device. This includes your track list, Series, Routines, listening progress, listening history, reminder times and app settings. It also stores which local folders you allowed it to read. This data is not sent to the developer.

3. Google user data

Google sign-in is optional. If you connect a Google account, the app asks only for the permissions needed for the feature you turn on. Each permission is asked for separately, at the moment you use that feature.

Permission (scope)Why the app uses it
openid, email, profileTo show which Google account is connected, and to keep each account's data separate.
drive.fileTo open audio files that you pick yourself with the Google Drive picker. The app can see only those files.
drive.readonlyOnly if you turn on the Root Audio Library. Lets the app list and play audio files in the Drive folder you choose. It is read-only: the app never changes, moves or deletes anything in your Drive.
drive.appdataOnly if you turn on sync. Stores the app's own sync records in a hidden app-data folder in your Drive, so your devices can share progress and routines.

What the sync records contain

Track names and details, Series and Routine setup, listening progress, finished listening history and resume points. They never contain audio files, device file paths, passwords, sign-in tokens or hardware identifiers.

How Google user data is handled

  • Data moves directly between your device and Google. It never passes through a server run by the developer.
  • The developer cannot see your Drive files, your sync records or your account details.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, inside the app, for you.
  • It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your data.

Hari Sumiran's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sign-in tokens

Sign-in tokens are kept only on your device, in the system's secure storage (Android secure storage, or Windows data protection for the signed-in user). They are never sent to the developer.

5. Device permissions

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show your listening reminders and the playback controls.
Exact alarms, run at start-upTo deliver reminders on time, also after the phone restarts.
Background media playbackTo keep playing when the screen is off or you switch apps.
InternetOnly for the optional Google Drive features.
Folder access you chooseTo read audio from local folders you pick. The app does not ask for broad storage access.

6. Your choices and deleting data

  • Disconnect Google: use the sign-out option in the app, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete sync data: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → Hari Sumiran / BhajanFlow → Delete hidden app data.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings.
  • Your audio files in Drive or on your device are never deleted by the app.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
